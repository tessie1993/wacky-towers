# User decisions, 2026-10-14

Source: the user's answers after the art, level and narrative directors studied the reference boards
(`assets/art/reference/index.md`). Supporting docs: `design/gdd/narrative/story-v2.md`,
`design/levels/mockup-alignment.md`, `design/art/art-gap-review-2026-10-13.md`.

| Topic | Decision |
|---|---|
| Pirates | Pirate Cove is the **11th main biome**, after Underwater. It has its own block skin, Kraken Blockade, Fort Blast and the Rainbow Kraken boss. Underwater gets a Crab King boss (Admiral Crab's claws). |
| Heroes | **Reskin** C2–C4 as Knight, Dwarf and Elf, keeping Lana's, Boulder's and Glim's perks. Lana, Boulder and Glim become story NPCs. Pirate, Robot, Astronaut, Explorer and Alien are later unlocks. |
| Story text | **Short captions are allowed.** Painted story panels get a title ribbon and a short caption. Tutorial bubbles are 12 words or fewer. In-level moments stay wordless. Text is live UI, never baked into the art. |
| Story scope | **All 20 painted story panels** (S01–S20 in story-v2 §7). |

Earlier standing rules still apply:
- No Nintendo IP.
- Never copy the board logos.
- Avoid "Tetris" naming.
- Story is an optional mode and never gates progress.
