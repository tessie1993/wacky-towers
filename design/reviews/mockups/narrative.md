## 0. Ground rules this review applies (all E)

1. **Wordless.** Story is told with skits, poses, props and emote bubbles. UI labels (scores, mode names) are UI, not story.
2. **Nobody is hurt.** Characters are only bonked, floured, dusted or rained on. Bosses are rivals, not monsters, and every boss prank is a misused party gift from Mizzle.
3. **Mascots stay home.** Bosses roam (they cameo in tournaments). Bosses are **never playable**. Mizzle appears in tournaments only after the finale.
4. **Story is skippable and costs no play time.** One intro skit (3 s or less) and one payoff skit (5 s or less) per level. There is at most one thread clue per level.
5. **Canon cast**:
   - The wizard (C1) and the friends Lana, Boulder and Glim (C2-C4).
   - Mascots: Pip, Mallow, Pebble, Puff, Cinder, Chip, Nugget, Tock, Glitch, Comet and Bounce.
   - Bosses: the Miller, Madame Meringue, Big Sniffles, Admiral Crab, Smolder, Grandpa Oak, Geode, Cuckoo Prime, DJ Mirrorball, the Sleepy Moon and Baron Balloon.
   - Party characters (from this brief): pirate, astronaut, robot, penguin, alien, tiki, monkey and parrot.

**A rule that follows from the module decision (P):** a campaign level made of 2-3 module phases still gets **one** intro and **one** payoff skit. The per-module story beats in section 2 are carried by **staging**: what the mascot is doing at the rim, what the boss is doing in the backdrop, and a single reaction bubble of 1 s or less at a phase change. They are never an extra skit, and a module never adds a second thread clue.

## 3. Boss phases: Kraken, Golem and Jungle Giant on canon bosses

The general principle (P, for game-designer): the boss phase shows the boss's **want being met**, not a health bar draining. The canon L10 cards already work this way: sails are knocked off, chimes are knocked off, bulbs dim, clock hands move, the pillow lifts. The mockups' "Boss Health Bar", "Fort HP", "Hull Integrity" and "Castress Health" become a **want meter** made of icons from that boss's own story.

| Mockup boss | Canon boss | How the phase maps | Want meter | Bonk (E) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Kraken** (Kraken Blockade, Abyssal Kraken Siege, Kraken Keep; idea bank "Inkwell") | **Admiral Crab** (Underwater) | **Recommended (P):** "the Fleet's kraken": Crab's hermit-crab crew wear a giant seaweed-and-shell tentacle costume, and Crab steers it from a shell howdah wearing his tiny hat. Each tentacle swipes a lane to snatch shiny blocks for the hoard. ## 4. Tournament framing

### 4.1 Options for the host (P)

| Option | Who hosts | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Baron Balloon, ringmaster (Recommended)** | The Tumble Fair boss, pompous, hovering over the arena | His canon want is "to be the biggest thing at the fair" (E), so hosting the contest **is** his want, which fits ludonarratively. The fair is already the canon tournament hub (E, brief section 4). Bosses roam and cameo in tournaments (E). He works wordlessly: he inflates on the leader and twirls his moustache | He is a boss before he is beaten, but bosses are rivals, so that is fine |
| B. Bounce, as mascot host | The baby elephant | Warm and cute | "Mascots stay home" is fine, since the fair is her home. But she cannot "host" rounds on other islands, and she is a watcher, not a showman |
| C. A neutral referee (the grumpy cloud) | The wizard's grumpy cloud | Already on screen | Breaks canon: "No existing behaviour changes", and its secret (Mizzle's lost mail-cloud) would be spent on a menu role |

**Recommended framing (A, all P except where marked E):**
- **Baron Balloon** is the ringmaster. **Bounce** spins the round-pick wheel with her trunk; the mockup's "Reroll (1)" button is her bonking it again.
- The **round's biome boss** pops in between rounds as a chaos cameo (E). With Items On, that boss's misused gift is the round's item, for example Smolder's bath bomb or Crab's claw.
- **After the finale** (E), Mizzle joins as friendly chaos co-host and the grand final moves to **Drizzle Rock: "Mizzle's Party"** (E).

### 4.2 Why head-to-head, and why random

- **Why h2h (in the world):** a fair runs contests. The Baron stages one so that he can hover over the prize and be the biggest thing in every photo.
- **Why h2h (in the story):** the brief's core twist is "the pranks are the party games he meant to host" (E). The tournament rounds **are** those party games, played as intended. Before the finale the player does not know this. After the finale, Mizzle co-hosts and the player sees it: an "I knew it" payoff that costs no play time.
- **Why random:** a fair's wheel of fortune. The round is pulled from the module pool and **keeps its biome skin** (idea bank rule 5), so every round is a short visit to an island. The local mascot watches from the rim, so mascots still stay home.
- **Ludonarrative check:** h2h rewards bonking a rival's tower, and the story says rivals are friends who misunderstand each other. That is consistent: every bonk is a gag, losers are rained on by the grumpy cloud (existing gag), and the round ends with a handshake pose, never a knockout.

### 4.3 Trophy flavour (P)

- **The Tumble Cup**: a gold cup (gold is the reward colour) with a small crooked tower of blocks on top that **wobbles on a spring**. It echoes the game's title and quietly echoes Mizzle's crooked tower. **It is never violet**: Drizzle Rock's cloud charm is a violet cup (E), and the two must not be confused.
- **Round-win rosettes** carry the **community stamp** of the round's biome (E stamps: leaf, sugar cube, snowflake, shell, ember, acorn, gem, cog, pixel heart, crescent). A tournament leaves a wordless strip of rosettes that reads as a trip round the islands. This replaces the coloured round-history dots in `tournament_flow_ref` step 5.
- **Match point**: the Baron drifts over the leader and inflates a little bigger. This replaces or sits beside the flame icon.
- **The win skit (5 s or less)**: the winner's cloud rises with the cup (as in the mockup). The Baron deflates a puff in envy before Tumble Fair is beaten, and bounces as a bouncy castle after (E, his bonk). Losers get a light grumpy-cloud drizzle with `sweat`, **never `tear`** (E rule 6).
- **Mizzle's Party final** (after the finale): the cup is presented by Mizzle, hat star lit, with a little bow.
- A trophy shelf in the Scrapbook would be **new scope**. It is flagged to producer and not proposed for MVP.
