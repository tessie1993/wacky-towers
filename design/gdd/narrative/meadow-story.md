# Meadow story: Pip's Picnic

> **Status**: Draft v1 (narrative-director, 2026-10-10), for user review. Tunable defaults.
> **Sources**: `design/levels/meadow.md` (wins every conflict on rules and beats), `production/levels/meadow/world-and-scenes.md`, `biome-stories.md`, user decisions 2026-10-10.
> **Changes to meadow.md beats**: only the **10 payoff** (revised, §4) and small **additions** (invites, Miller clues) to existing payoffs. No rule or number changes. All listed in §6.

## 1. The arc in one paragraph

Pip wants a picnic on the hilltop **with everyone**, and hands out leaf invites to every friend it helps. The cloud wizard drops the blocks that make it all possible. The Miller up the hill keeps spoiling things with gusts, spores, fog, flour and a giant lever. Pip thinks he is a bully. Attentive players see more: the Miller picks up a stray invite that is not for him, sets out one cup, dreams of a picnic, and nearly waves. In 10 the last clear bonks him into flour, his own tiny one-badger blanket flutters down beside Pip's, and Pip finally understands. Pip hands him an invite. He blushes, sits at the edge of the picnic, sneezes flour on the sandwiches, and everyone laughs. He hands the wizard the tiny windmill as thanks.

**Day cycle (recommended, F1)**: three days, read from the light alone. Day 1 = 01–02 (morning, bedtime), Day 2 = 03–06 (wind to late afternoon), Day 3 = 07–10 (dawn fog to golden-hour picnic). The picnic keeps getting postponed; the golden light in 10 is the "finally".

**Why the clues work**: each is a surface emote slipping into a secret one, in the backdrop of a payoff the player is already watching. Missing every clue still leaves a clean story (a prankster who gets invited); catching them turns 10 into "I knew it". Clue density rises 03 → 09, and the 💧 `tear` is used only for him, so its meaning is learnt.

## 3. The keepsake

The **tiny windmill** (Established). New framing: the Miller made it; it sits on his roof beside his tiny blanket in every close-up of the mill (05 spyglass, 10). In the 10 payoff he hands it up to the wizard as thanks, and it pops onto the hat brim (the growing-hat gag). It is a gift, not a trophy, which matches the redemption.

## 4. Revised meadow_10 payoff (proposed)

Replaces meadow.md §6 "10 Payoff skit" (the stomp-off). ≤ 6 s first play, the last 2 beats on replays.

1. The last clear: the Miller sails off the roof into a flour cloud (kept). He climbs out white from ears to tail, shakes a fist (`angry`), turns to stomp off (kept, as a fake-out).
2. His tiny one-badger blanket flutters off the roof and lands beside Pip's big blanket. Pip looks at it, at him: `question` → `exclaim`.
3. Pip runs over and holds out an invite (`give` pose, `invite`). The Miller freezes: `dots` → `blush`. He takes it.
4. He sits at the edge of the picnic, still floury; the friends shuffle up to make room (`heart`). He sneezes flour over the sandwiches; everyone laughs (`note`).
5. He looks up to the wizard and holds up the tiny windmill; it flies to the hat brim (`sparkle`). A last cheeky wink-and-fist-shake at the wizard: friendly rivals.

## 5. Per-level skit briefs (for the writer)

Format: **I** = intro (≤ 3 s, in the countdown, ends on the hand-off), **P** = payoff (≤ 5 s), **M** = Miller clue (backdrop, inside P), **R** = key reactions. Writer scripts the beats; timings are defaults.

**01 First Sprout**
- I: Pip digs, drops the seed, pats it, looks up at the drizzle, then at the wizard (`question`).
- P: The sprout shoots up into a sunflower; Pip hangs the basket on it, plucks a leaf and stamps it with its paw: the first invite (`idea`).
- M: none. Mill far, sails idle.
- R: clear `heart`; warning Pip cover-eyes + `sweat`; catch `exclaim`.

**02 Tilt & Roll**
- I: Three friends in nightcaps stare at the holes; one lies in a hole, sticking out both ends; Pip looks up (`question`).
- P: Each friend flops into a bed and snores (`sleep`); Pip tucks an invite under each pillow, shushes the wizard (`dots`), blows out the candle.
- M: none.
- R: pocket filled, friend `sleep`; wrong piece Pip "nope" wobble.

**03 Breezy Hill**
- I: A gust blows the seeds (and one invite) out of Pip's paws; Pip tumbles after them (`angry`).
- P: The wind dies; seeds land in neat rows; Pip plants the last one with a flourish.
- M: Far uphill, the Miller's silhouette catches the stray invite, looks at it, `tear`, pockets it, cranks on.
- R: gust telegraph Pip `exclaim`; seeds on Pip's whiskers, sneeze.

**04 Mushroom Ring**
- I: Pip lays the cloth by the pond; a mushroom pops under it and launches it into the water (`exclaim`).
- P: Mushrooms line up and bow; Pip bounces across them to fetch the cloth and hands the frogs an invite; the frogs croak together (`note`).
- M: The bellows wheeze to a stop as the frogs croak; the Miller `dots`, then pumps again.
- R: sparkle telegraph Pip `exclaim`; mushroom blocks a gap, mushroom grins.

**05 Tall Tower**
- I: Pip jumps to see over the grass, can't, draws a tower in the dirt (`idea`).
- P: Pip climbs, raises a spyglass, spots the Miller cranking a giant fan; ears flat (`angry`).
- M: The spyglass circle holds one beat on his door: one cup, one chair, the tiny windmill on the roof.
- R: trim, Miller laughs `smug` in the backdrop; Big Cube "thunk" Pip `exclaim`.

**06 Flower Bed**
- I: Pip looks at an empty vase, then the bare bed, draws a flower outline (`idea`).
- P: The flower blooms; Pip pulls and the whole bed comes up as one giant bouquet (`sparkle`).
- M: The Miller naps (`sleep`); his dream bubble shows the `invite` icon, then pops.
- R: petal trimmed, Pip chases it; sprout grows Pip `note`.

**07 Hide & Seek**
- I: Pip sets the basket on the stack, turns, the fog swallows it; Pip pats the air (`question`).
- P: The last clear blows the fog away; the basket was on Pip's head all along; Pip invites the waking owl.
- M: The fog lifts off the bank: the Miller caught peeking, invite corner in his pocket; `exclaim`, scuttles into the mist.
- R: fade, Pip ears out of the fog `question`; clear, Pip pops out `exclaim`.

**08 Dewdrop**
- I: Pip steps on the grass and is glued; feet stretch like taffy and snap back (`sweat`).
- P: The dew sparkles away; Pip pops free and somersaults into the basket (`sparkle`).
- M: On his doorstep, chin on paws, the Miller claps once as Pip pops free, `dots`, hides behind the door.
- R: splat lock Pip wriggles; warning `sweat`.

**09 Topsy-Turvy**
- I: The Miller yanks the lever; the hill turns over; Pip is left hanging from a root (`dizzy`).
- P: The hill flips back; Pip drops onto the grass, the chicks drop onto Pip; a dizzy, happy pile (`heart`).
- M: The Miller watches the pile cuddle: `tear`, then `angry`, and yanks the lever extra hard (it creaks: foreshadowing 10).
- R: flip warning all `exclaim`; after a flip `dizzy`; egg hatches chick `heart`.

**10 Meadow Mill**
- I: Pip spreads the blanket; the Miller slams the shutter, cranks his sails, and the belt rolls the blanket away (`smug`).
- P: See §4 (the redemption).
- M: (it is the payoff).
- R: warning, Miller dances on the roof `smug`; each sail lost, Miller `gloom`; phase 2 lever, Miller `angry`; Pip `heart` on each clear.

**B Picnic Puzzle**
- I: An ant scout spots the food and whistles; a long ant line turns toward the cloth (`exclaim`).
- P: The lid snaps shut as the ants arrive; they bonk into the basket one after another; Pip sits on the lid (`smug`).
- R: ants near, Pip `sweat`; fail, the ants march off with the basket.

**H1–H3** (brief; scripts later): H1 the sunflower sneezes seeds, sprouts everywhere; payoff Pip pots one seed and lids it. H2 an ant bites the bottom sandwich; payoff the colony leaves with one crumb. H3 Pip skids across the plank between two patches; payoff two giant carrots flatten Pip. Recommended: the Miller waves from the backdrop in all three.
