> **Sources**: `biome-stories.md` (cast), `world-and-scenes.md` §1 and §3 C (poses), `art-bible.md` §5 (wizard archetype, expression), user decisions 2026-10-10.
> Visual notes are **input for the art-director**, not decisions. Emote ids: `emote-bubbles.md`.

## The cloud wizard (the player)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Role** | The player's avatar. Sits on a cloud just off the board and casts the block drizzle; every piece that falls is the wizard's. Never on the grid. Collects the Keepsake on its hat at the end of each biome |
| **Want** | To help. Never stated; shown by how the friends treat it (they look up, wave, ask) |
| **Personality** | Kindly, playful, a little clumsy. Proud of good play, sheepish about bad drops |
| **Silhouette** | Small, round, a big cone hat, big beard, wand, on one soft cloud shape (art bible §5). Meadow costume suggestion: a daisy in the hat band and a gingham neckerchief; the cone hat and cloud stay the same |
| **Expression** | Brows, eyes and hat carry emotion (beard hides the mouth): the hat stands up when happy, droops when sad. Key poses: idle bob, cast, cheer, dismay, shock |
| **Gags** | The **grumpy cloud** rains on the wizard on a loss. The **growing hat** gets the tiny windmill after 10 |
| **Emotes** | `sweat`, `sparkle`, `note`, `heart`, `exclaim`, `idea`, `gloom` (loss only) |
| **Story function** | Pip's intro skits end by looking up to the wizard for help (the hand-off). In 10 the wizard is who Pip turns to, and the Miller hands the keepsake *to the wizard*, so the player is thanked directly |

## Pip (the friend you help)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Role** | The Meadow mascot and heart of the story. Helper in 01–03 (points at a cell, one catch), watcher from 04. Never pranks (meadow.md) |
| **Want** | A picnic on the hilltop **with everyone**. Pip hands out invites from 02 on; "everyone" is the point, which is what makes the Miller's exclusion sting, and Pip fixing it the payoff |
| **Personality** | Tiny, brave, busy, a planner. Bounces back from every bonk. Misreads the Miller as a bully until 10 |
| **Silhouette** | A harvest mouse with huge round ears and a long curly tail, carrying a picnic basket twice its size (the basket is its silhouette hook). Ears are its main tell: up = alert, flat = cross, drooped = sad |
| **Poses** | idle, point, catch, cheer, cover-eyes, sneeze, stuck, hang (world-and-scenes C1), plus two new: **look-up** (the hand-off) and **give** (holding out an invite) |
| **Emotes** | `heart`, `exclaim`, `question`, `idea`, `note`, `sparkle`, `sweat`, `angry` (at gusts and the Miller), `dizzy`, `invite`, `tear` (loss only) |

## The Miller (rival, secretly lonely)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Role** | Causes every disturbance from 03 (sails, bellows, chimney, flour, lever, belt); the boss in 10 |
| **Surface want** | A quiet mill and loud gusts (biome-stories). He hates party *noise* |
| **Secret want** | To be invited. The noise he hates is the sound of a party he is not in. His pranks are the only way he knows to join in |
| **Personality** | Gruff, proud, smug when a prank lands, shy underneath. Covers every soft moment by snapping back to smug |
| **Silhouette** | A big, round, flour-dusted badger: miller's cap, apron, the white face stripe (it reads at phone size). Large enough to read as a boss at a third of the screen in 10 |
| **The tell** | When lonely he fiddles with his cap. A **single cup** and **one chair** sit by his mill door. He keeps the invite that blew away in 03 in his apron pocket from then on (a corner of leaf sticking out) |
| **Poses** | crank, lever, cheer, sulk, laugh, bonk (world-and-scenes C2), plus new: **peek**, **nap**, **almost-wave**, **sit**, **receive** |
| **Emotes** | Surface: `smug`, `angry`, `gloom`. Secret: `dots`, `tear`, `blush`. End: `heart`, `note` |
| **Arc** | Prankster → bonked → invited → sits at the picnic, still floury. Still a rival in spirit (a cheeky fist-shake to the wizard), so "rivals return" keeps working, but as a friendly rival |
