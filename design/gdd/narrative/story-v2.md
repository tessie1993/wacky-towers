**Composition rules read from the story boards** (used in section 7):
1. Wide painted scene, warm key light, rim light and **floating magic motes** (stars and sparkles) around anything magical.
2. **Wizard, book and blocks are the three recurring props.** The blocks glow and are the brightest saturated thing in frame.
3. Characters are chunky, big-headed, toy-like and expressive; heads at one third of frame height at most in wide shots.
4. **Frame template**: purple-wood vine frame (tutorial and settings), or full-bleed painting with a **bottom ribbon title** in thick cream caps with dark outline. Chapter panels add a top **plaque** ("2. THE STORY BEGINS:").
5. Text lives in three containers only: plaque (chapter), ribbon (title), parchment scroll or speech bubble (narration or tutor line).

## 1. The premise

### 1.1 The story in one page

**Core concept**: The towers that once held the world's floating islands up fell long ago and took their spellbooks, the **Lost Tomes**, with them. The old cloud wizard and a ragtag crew of builders ride a block-cart island to island, rebuild a tower at each stop with the wizard's block drizzle, win back a Tome from whoever is sitting on it, and finally open the Cosmic Gateway to stand the towers up again.

**History**:
- *Long ago*: the magical towers stood on every island, each holding a Tome that told its island how to float. In the **Great Toppling** the towers fell, the Tomes scattered, and the islands began to drift apart.
- *The wizard's library*: one island kept its library. The wizard has watched over it alone, casting the one spell that survived, the **block drizzle** (blocks of whatever the island is made of), which is the only thing that can stack a tower back up.
- *Now*: the oldest Tome opens by itself and shows a tower of light. "The story begins." The wizard sends the first builders out.

**Rules of the story**:
1. **The player is the wizard.** You cast every block. Heroes are the hands that carry and place them (they appear in minigames and on the cart). The wizard never acts against input.
2. Every biome holds **one Tome** (10 Tomes plus the Pirate Island Tome as a bonus chapter, section 4.11). It is **won back, not stolen**: the holder is a rival who has misunderstood what the Tome is for (section 3).
3. Why the drizzle is blocks is **never explained** (existing law, `contradictions-resolved.md` C16). Why islands float is explained only by the towers.
4. **Nobody is hurt.** Goblins, guardians and krakens are bonked, doused, tickled or put to sleep. Block-cannons fire blocks at **walls, towers and bosses' gadgets, never at people**.
5. Story is a **mode**, optional, skippable, and never gates the Campaign, Tournament or Arcade (the anti-pillar "NOT a story-driven game" now reads: story is available and free, never forced).

**Player relevance**: the Story mode card opens the six-chapter quest (sections 4 and 7); Campaign biomes borrow its places and characters; the Tournament uses the cast as contestants.

### 1.2 Premise in three sentences

Long ago the magical towers fell and their spellbooks, the Lost Tomes, were scattered across the floating islands. The old cloud wizard, with a knight, a dwarf, an elf, a pirate and their friends riding a block-cart, sets out to rebuild the towers one stack at a time and win each Tome back from the rival who is sitting on it. At the Cosmic Gateway the last Tome opens the way home, the towers rise, and the party finds the treasure the towers were guarding all along.

### 1.4 The Mizzle thread, folded into the Tomes (optional)

To avoid throwing away the finished thread, v2 **merges** it rather than running two plots:
- Mizzle (the lonely rival wizard) is the one who **found the Lost Tomes first**. He could not carry them back, so he mailed each Tome, folded into a party-gift parcel tied with a **violet ribbon**, to the island's holder. Each holder misread the gift and sat on it. The violet ribbon is the Tome's **bookmark** (the 10 flag panels are the 10 bookmarks).
- Therefore every boss is "a rival who misunderstood a gift", and the Tome is inside the gift. The existing flag, cuff, chair and crooked-tower clues stay as backdrop props and cost nothing.
- Mizzle appears only in the Celestial finale and (post-game) Drizzle Rock, as written in the campaign bible.
- **If the user drops the thread**, nothing breaks: the violet ribbon becomes a plain Tome ribbon and Mizzle simply never appears. Section 4 marks his beats as `[B-story]`.

## 4. Beat sheets

**Delivery codes**
- **[P]** painted story panel (section 7). Chapter panels use the plaque, ribbon and scroll caption; played once, skippable, replayable from Story mode.
- **[3D]** in-level 3D moment: a staged camera move or prop reveal inside the board's scene. No caption, or an emote bubble only.
- **[TB]** tutorial bubble: an in-world speech bubble with a hand cursor, tied to a tutor character.
- **[SK]** the existing wordless skit in `campaign_skits.json`, played in the 3 s countdown and 5 s payoff.

Common rule: a level gets one intro and one payoff at most; a mid beat is a **[3D]** moment; the story never costs play time and never sits over the grid. The wizard reacts to the player's play.

## 5. The tutorial flow

Source: board 07 (First Steps; Balance and physics), board 01 Q2 (Learn to Stack), board 02 #1 (The Tutorial: Drag and Match).

**Set**: a **floating sky island** with a pale grid on grass, the wizard on a cloud at upper right, the knight at the lower left, the pirate on a tilting plate later. All teaching is **in-world speech bubbles with a hand cursor**, on the real board, never a text card. Painted tutorial cards do not exist; the island is the lesson.

**Tutors**
| Tutor | Teaches | Placement of the bubble |
|---|---|---|
| **Knight** | Placement: tap a cell, place a block, build up | Lower left, scroll-style bubble, as in board 07 |
| **Wizard** | Magic: rotate (the Rotate button), drag and match, ghost block preview | Upper right on his cloud, as in boards 01 and 02 |
| **Pirate** | Balance and physics: the tilt plate, wind arrows, the balance meter | On the plate, bubble to the left, as in board 07 |

**Flow** (each step skippable; the whole tutorial is skippable and shown once per profile; step counts and the lesson order are game-designer's call)
| Step | Lesson | Tutor + draft bubble (writer to polish) | Bubble type |
|---|---|---|---|
| T1 First Steps | Tap to place a block on the glowing cell | Knight: "Tap and place the block to build your tower!" | **[TB]** |
| T2 Rotate | Turn the piece | Wizard: "Tap to rotate, drag to drop!" | **[TB]** |
| T3 Drag and Match | Match the ghost block, drop it into the highlighted cell | Wizard: "Drag and match!" | **[TB]** |
| T4 Clear a layer | Fill a layer and watch it settle | Knight: "A full row settles. Nice work!" | **[TB]** |
| T5 Balance and Physics | The plate tilts; keep the tower level; wind pushes | Pirate: "Watch the wind!" | **[TB]** |
| T6 Graduation | Tower stands; the first Tome page glows | Wizard: wand glint, no bubble | **[3D]** |

**Rules**
1. One bubble at a time, **12 words or fewer**, never over the grid, never covering the next piece.
2. The hand cursor is the primary signal; the bubble is the support.
3. Reduced motion: bubbles appear without bounce; the cursor still moves.
4. Tutor characters are the real roster characters, so players learn the faces they will meet.
5. The tutorial island sits at the edge of the Meadow map as "level zero"; it is not required for Meadow progress if the profile has already completed it once.

## 6. The tournament: Tumble Fair and the Fair Plaza

### 6.1 Tumble Fair (board 14)
**Core concept**: a lantern-lit fairground that floats between the Meadow and Candy. **Baron Balloon** is the ringmaster; **Bounce** the baby elephant spins the mode wheel.

**Flow, with story beats** (the five frames of board 14)
| Frame | UI | Story layer |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Setup and lobby | 4 apprentice wizards (circle, triangle, square, diamond), rounds 3/5/7/13, match rule "first to 3 wins", Normal/Mixed, items on/off, Ready | Baron's pennant banner reads the Fair's name (a title in the Fair's own sign, not the logo); each Ready player's apprentice waves from a cloud seat; Baron: "Welcome to the Tumble Fair!" |
| 2 Round pick | Wheel with 3 modes (Clear Race, Highest Tower, Largest Footprint), Reroll, countdown | Bounce pushes the wheel; the Baron's balloon bobs overhead; a lantern marks Reroll |
| 3 Round play | Four floating islands, four towers, mode banner | The apprentice (or chosen roster character) builds; the crowd in the distance |
| 4 Standings | Rank, wins, "Match point!" flame | Baron reads the board from his gondola; trophies on the banner |
| 5 Tournament win | Winner on a cloud with a trophy, round history | The winner's apprentice lifts the golden trophy; confetti; Baron bows |

**Baron's role**: pompous but kind. His Tumble Fair campaign story ends with him deflating into a bouncy castle (existing); he is **reinflated as ringmaster** and from then on is the host. He never fights in the tournament; he **reacts** (a one-line quip at the start, the match-point and the win). Quips are text on a banner in the fair and are skippable.

**Voice**: Baron is theatrical and warm. Draft lines: start "Welcome to the Tumble Fair!", match point "Match point! The crowd goes wild!", win "A champion! Bravo!"

**Rules**
1. **4 players maximum.** Badges circle, triangle, square, diamond; star is a reward.
2. Tension: a single party track and a tension pulse in the last 10 seconds (decision).
3. Bosses cameo as **chaos sources** between rounds (existing); Mizzle only after the finale.
4. Baron never appears in the Campaign levels as an antagonist after Tumble Fair is done.

## 7. Story-panel production list

**Panel template** (all panels):
- Size 4:3 landscape (matches the landscape-first decision); a portrait variant of any panel is optional later. Export at 2048 x 1536.
- **Painted scene in the reference style**: warm key light, rim light, glowing magic motes, chunky toy characters, a clear block-glow focal point (boards 01, 02, 07).
- **Containers**: top plaque (chapter, optional), bottom **ribbon title** (thick cream caps, dark outline), parchment **scroll caption** (optional, 12 words or fewer).
- The text is **live UI**, not baked into the art, so it can be localized. Art leaves clean space for the ribbon (bottom 18 percent) and the scroll (lower third on the left or right).
- No logos from any board. No Nintendo-adjacent designs. No flat vector style.
- Delivery for all panels: **[P]**, played once, tap to advance, a Skip control, replayable from the Story mode storybook.
