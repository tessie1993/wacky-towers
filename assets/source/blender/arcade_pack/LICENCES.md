# Toy-Box Trials asset provenance

All geometry and matte materials in this folder and its three exported GLBs were
created originally for Wacky Towers by the game suite art team. No downloaded
models, fonts, image textures, HDRIs, generative images, or third-party materials
are embedded. These new files follow the repository's MIT licence (`/LICENSE`).

Blender 4.5.9 LTS (build `8bf95cbd38d1`) is the authoring tool. Blender's own GPL
licence applies to Blender, not to this original generated artwork.

The art bible's existing inspiration references were used only as guidance:
quiet matte floating-island dioramas, broad colour planes, organic outlines and
exterior story props. No reference artwork was traced or copied.
