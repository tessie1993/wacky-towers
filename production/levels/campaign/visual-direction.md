# Campaign visual direction and 100-level staging

As built on 2026-10-10. Source-provenanced original geometry is generated by `WtToyActor` and `WtStage`; wordless scenes are compiled by `tools/content/generate_stories.py`. This document records the actual realization rather than promising a final studio art pack.

## Readability and spatial grammar

The active grid keeps one shared bevelled cube mesh and palette identities. Active, locked, ghost, landing, fit suggestions and rule telegraphs use MultiMeshes. Quiet furniture is outside the playable grid; a level hero prop and a smaller invitation clue occupy opposite rim positions. Ten fixed approach/depth slots vary with the authored level number, without randomizing puzzle data. Camera framing reserves the production HUD and touch-control areas. No final geometry uses animated CSG.

Meadow uses seven authored light situations: first morning, bedtime, breezy day, late afternoon, dawn fog, morning and golden picnic. Fog rules fade locked contents using coverage dithering while the active piece and exact ghost stay readable. Other biomes retain their matte island palette and original regional props; their per-level hero/clue staging changes with the source story. Background colours are quieter than pieces and warning overlays.

The original cast has 25 forms: four playable friends, Mizzle, ten mascots and ten local bosses. Pip is a round-eared harvest mouse with a basket; Lana has a tall neck, wool and knitting needles; Boulder has a broad body, lamp and mallet; Glim hangs upside down with a blueprint; Mizzle has the hooked violet hat, pale-gold glasses, scarf and satchel. These are procedural toy sculptures with shared poses and installed Beehave reaction/ambient trees. They are not a skeleton animation pack.
