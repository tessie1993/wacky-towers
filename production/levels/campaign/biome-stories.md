# Biome Mini Stories (Campaign, 10 biomes)

> **Status**: Draft v1 for user review (narrative-director, 2026-10-10)
> **Owner**: narrative-director. Level designers design FROM the beats here; mechanics stay with game-designer / level-designer.
> **Everything here is a tunable default.** Names, species and beats are starting points.
> **Anti-pillar respected**: no story campaign. No dialogue, no cutscene walls. Stories are told by (a) the level goal, (b) mascot/boss reactions, (c) the island itself and its underside (art bible: wordless storytelling), (d) a one-image "storybook card" at most before a level (optional, picture only).

## Tone rules

1. **Wordless first.** If a beat needs a sentence to be understood, redesign the level goal or the mascot's pose. Max 3 words on any card ("Build a bridge!").
2. **Wacky test every level**: surprising rule + silly object + funny failure + big visual moment.
3. **Humour mix**: wholesome slapstick (bonks, tumbles, splats) / dry-absurd (a very serious penguin, a boss with a tiny grievance) / cheeky chaos (pests, pranks, the boss cheering when you fail).
4. **Failure is a gag, never a scolding.** Trims, warnings and losses are the mascot's funniest animation. Nobody is ever hurt, only bonked and dusty.
5. **Bosses are proper rivals** (user decision, 2026-10-10). Every boss has a small, silly want and stays smug. The finale beats them, but they never join the party: they stomp off vowing a rematch and return as recurring opponents (see "Rivals return"). They are rivals, not monsters, so they are bonked and never hurt.
6. **The story is the level goal.** Clear / Height / Shape / Survive each read as an action (clean up, build up, make a picture, hang on). Design the level so the goal IS the story beat.
7. **Block weather is the only "why".** Blocks fall because it rains blocks here; each biome has its own forecast (see cast). Nobody explains it.
8. **Mascot mood follows level and difficulty** (helper / prankster / mood swing / watcher), per user decision.

## Recurring cast

**The Wizard** (art bible: kindly toy-box owner on a cloud, cone hat, big beard, wand). Mute, expressive, never on the board. Each biome dresses them in a costume. Arc across the campaign (user decision): the wizard collects a **Keepsake** from each biome, a tiny charm that appears on the hat brim after each finale. The hat grows a little taller and odder with each one. The 10th Keepsake completes the hat in Celestial, and that is the big absurd reveal: **the tower was the wizard's hat all along** (see Celestial). The arc is never stated; it is a growing hat that players read in hindsight.

**Mascots own their biomes** (user decision). Pip appears in the Meadow only, and each biome's mascot is the only mascot there. The one exception is Celestial, the finale biome, where every mascot gathers for the reveal.

**Mascot behaviour** depends on the level and the difficulty: helper, prankster, mood swing or watcher (user decision). The personality line in the table sets how each behaviour is played. For example, Pebble the prankster is deadpan, while Puff the prankster is dramatic.

**Rivals return** (user decision). After its finale, each boss stays smug and comes back as a recurring opponent:
- **Remix levels**: a boss crashes another biome's level and brings its own event. For example, the Miller's gusts blow in the Ice biome, or Admiral Crab steals blocks in Lava. This uses the user's "biome events remix into later biomes" rule.
- **Hard-track levels**: a rematch on the boss's home island with one extra trick.
- **Tournament cameos**: a boss turns up as a chaos event or a sabotage source in minigames, and it heckles whoever is in the lead.
- **The rival board**: each defeated boss watches from the edge of later finales, holding up a score sign and sulking.

**Running gags**
- **The rubber duck** hides on the underside of every island; finding it is an optional wink.
- **Boss bonk**: every boss leaves its finale with a different slapstick bonk (a flour cloud, a cake-topper plop, a sneeze-launch...). It always lands, dusts itself off and shakes a fist with a rematch promise.
- **The growing hat**: each Keepsake makes the wizard's hat taller and stranger, and nobody comments on it, until Celestial.
- **The grumpy cloud**: a tiny cloud on the wizard's cloud that rains on the wizard whenever you fail.

## 1. MEADOW: Pip's Picnic

**Mini story**
1. Pip the mouse wants a picnic on top of the hill with all the meadow friends.
2. The wizard's block drizzle falls from a sunny sky and Pip begs for a lookout, a bridge, a flower bouquet and a basket.
3. The Miller up the hill likes it quiet and rigs his mill to blow gusts, toss mushrooms, fog the grass and, in the end, flip the hill upside-down.
4. Everything goes wrong in tiny ways: Pip is blown into the dandelions, stuck in dew, hung from the upside-down tree.
5. Finale: the player stacks the mill's belt and lets the last clear bonk the Miller off his roof into a flour cloud.
6. Pip's picnic starts. The Miller climbs out of the flour, sneers at the picnic and stomps off to plan a rematch. Keepsake: a tiny windmill.

**10-beat arc** (respects meadow.md; no mechanical changes)
1. **First Sprout**: Pip finds a seed and needs a tidy plot; each clear makes the sprout grow a leaf.
2. **Tilt & Roll**: Pip's stone burrow has odd-shaped bedroom holes; fit the three funny pieces so everyone gets a bed.
3. **Breezy Hill**: dandelion gusts blow Pip's seeds along the hill; build with the wind, not against it.
4. **Mushroom Ring**: mushrooms with faces keep popping up around the pond; Pip bounces on them, they give free blocks.
5. **Tall Tower**: Pip wants a lookout to spy on the mill; stack to the wooden sign.
6. **Flower Bed**: Pip's bouquet for the picnic; plant the picture, petals bloom as cells fill.
7. **Hide & Seek**: morning fog swallows the stack; Pip plays peek-a-boo with it.
8. **Dewdrop**: sticky dew glues everything and Pip, so hold the line for 2:30.
9. **Topsy-Turvy**: the Miller flips the hill; Pip hangs upside-down and the tree grows downward.
10. **Meadow Mill (boss)**: the Miller winds up gusts, belt and mushrooms, cheers your trims, sulks on clears; the last clear bonks him off the mill.
- **Bonus: Picnic Puzzle**: Pip sits in the basket and dodges each drop; a perfect fill closes the lid, a failure spills the picnic.

**Changes to meadow.md (flagged)**: none to rules or numbers. Adds only names: the critter becomes **Pip** (harvest mouse), the Miller becomes a **badger**. Open: the Miller badger is a working choice.

## 2. CANDY: Mallow's Birthday Cake

**Mini story**
1. Mallow the marshmallow bunny wants to bake Granny a giant birthday cake.
2. It hails gumdrops, so the wizard stacks them as the cake's tiers.
3. Everything is sticky: syrup rivers slow pieces, jelly wobbles, a sugar rush makes Mallow bounce off the screen.
4. The cake keeps melting in the sun and the chocolate fountain pours sideways.
5. Finale: Madame Meringue, a towering meringue, wants the top spot and tries to sit on the cake.
6. The final clear plops her on top as the cake topper, upside-down. Granny blows out the candles, and the meringue fumes and swears revenge. Keepsake: a cherry.

**10-beat arc**
1. **Sugar Cube Start**: Mallow needs a foundation; clear sugar-cube layers to lay the cake base.
2. **Gumdrop Hail**: gumdrops pelt the board and stick; fill the cake-tin pockets shaped like the pieces.
3. **Syrup River**: a lollipop-lane island where syrup slows pieces and they glide home.
4. **Jelly Wobble**: a jelly bridge bounces landed pieces; Mallow bounces on it, pieces re-settle.
5. **Cake Tiers**: build up the tower to the candles; each tier is a different frosting colour.
6. **Icing Picture**: pipe "Happy Birthday!" as a flat target shape in icing.
7. **Gummy Worms**: cheeky gummy worms wriggle over the stack and nibble it; clear them before they get away with a block.
8. **Sugar Rush**: Mallow eats too much candy; everything speeds up; survive 2:30.
9. **Chocolate Fountain**: the fountain tilts and gravity pours sideways; the cake leans like a happy tower.
10. **Madame Meringue (transformation)**: meringue keeps dropping on the stack; clear to cancel her, the final clear turns her into the topper (upside-down, furious).
- **Bonus: Candy Box**: pack a box of sweets with a fixed piece list; Mallow tries to eat one.

## 3. FOREST: Chip's Bridge

**Mini story**
1. Chip, a baby beaver, wants to cross the stream to the berry patch before bedtime.
2. Acorns rain from the sky; the wizard uses them to build the beaver lodge and a bridge.
3. Squirrels steal acorns, fireflies refuse to shine, leaves blow everywhere, and roots wriggle under the stack.
4. Chip gnaws things that should not be gnawed.
5. Finale: Grandpa Oak, a cranky ancient tree, is trying to nap and keeps shaking the bridge.
6. The last clear bonks him and he blooms into embarrassing pink blossoms that form the bridge. He grumbles that he will be back once the blossoms fall. Keepsake: an acorn.

**10-beat arc**
1. **Acorn Alley**: Chip collects acorns and needs a neat pile; clear layers into the acorn shelf.
2. **Hollow Log**: Chip's family log has pocket-holes shaped like the pieces; fit them to build rooms.
3. **Bridge Builders**: the baby beavers need a bridge; build a shape across the stream.
4. **Squirrel Heist**: cheeky squirrels toss acorns that land on the stack; use them as free blocks or clear them before they steal one.
5. **Treehouse**: Chip's dream treehouse; build up to the sign (and Chip waves from the top).
6. **Firefly Night**: fireflies light only the nearby blocks; build in the glow.
7. **Falling Leaves**: wind of leaves pushes pieces; Chip rides the leaves with a wave.
8. **Woodpecker Knock**: a woodpecker knocks the stack at random; survive 2:30 without collapse.
9. **Root Wiggle**: roots slide the stack sideways along a belt; time your placement.
10. **Grandpa Oak (transformation)**: the tree shakes the bridge when you cheer too loud; each clear makes him shakier until he blooms (much to his disgust) and becomes the bridge.
- **Bonus: Berry Basket**: fill the basket with a fixed piece list while Chip eats the berries.

## 4. UNDERWATER: Puff's Pearl

**Mini story**
1. Puff the pufferfish wants to return a giant glowing pearl to its clam before sunset.
2. Blocks sink down like slow bubbles; the wizard wears diving goggles and builds a reef.
3. Currents shove the pieces, jellyfish bounce them, an octopus fogs the water, a whirlpool spins the reef.
4. Puff inflates at every surprise and bobs up to the surface.
5. Finale: Admiral Crab keeps stealing blocks and the pearl for "the fleet".
6. The final clear pops the pearl back into the clam and Admiral Crab shoots out in a bubble-bonk, then shakes a claw: "the fleet will return". Keepsake: a pearl.

**10-beat arc**
1. **Bubble Float**: pieces sink slowly; Puff puffs at the first clear and floats up.
2. **Clam Pockets**: clams open with pocket-shaped holes; fill them for pearls.
3. **Current Lane**: an underwater lane current pushes sideways; build with the current.
4. **Jellyfish Bounce**: jellyfish pop-ups bounce pieces off; use them as springy corners.
5. **Coral Tower**: grow a reef tower to the sign for Puff's friends.
6. **Pearl Necklace**: fill the necklace-shaped target; pearls glow as they fill.
7. **Ink Cloud**: an octopus squirts ink; the stack vanishes until you clear.
8. **Sinking Ship**: the water rises; survive 2:30 before the reef floods.
9. **Whirlpool**: gravity spirals and flips; fish swirl around the reef.
10. **Admiral Crab (boss duel)**: he steals blocks with his claw; clear to fend him off and drop the pearl into the clam.
- **Bonus: Treasure Chest**: fill a chest with a fixed piece list; Puff sits on top and inflates when you miss.

## 5. ICE: Pebble's Egg Escape

**Mini story**
1. Pebble the penguin wants to bring a snowball egg home to the igloo before the party.
2. It snows blocks; the wizard in a fluffy coat helps build an igloo and a snowman.
3. Everything slides, rolls and cracks: snowballs grow, blizzards blank the view, thin ice fails.
4. Pebble stays deadpan while falling over.
5. Finale: Big Sniffles the yeti sneezes an avalanche down the mountain.
6. Pebble, egg and wizard ride a sled you build ahead of the avalanche; the last jump lands home. The yeti sneezes himself over the horizon, smugly sure he won. Keepsake: a snowflake.

**10-beat arc**
1. **Snowflake Start**: Pebble stacks the first igloo bricks; clear layers.
2. **Icicle Pockets**: icicle-shaped pockets in the starter ice need the right pieces.
3. **Slippery Slide**: pieces slide after landing; Pebble slides across the island on his belly.
4. **Snowball Roll**: spawned snowballs roll and grow; steer them into pockets.
5. **Igloo Tower**: build a tower igloo to the sign; Pebble waves from the top.
6. **Snowman Shape**: fill a snowman target; scarf and hat are the last cells.
7. **Blizzard**: the whiteout hides the stack for brief spells; keep the shape in mind.
8. **Thin Ice**: ice cracks under the stack; survive 2:30 while the board shrinks.
9. **Ice Flip**: a crack flips the iceberg; the tower hangs from the top.
10. **Avalanche (escape)**: build a track for the sled before the avalanche reaches it; Big Sniffles sneezes at you.
- **Bonus: Present Puzzle**: wrap a present with a fixed piece list; Pebble guards it stoically.

## 6. CAVE: Nugget's Light

**Mini story**
1. Nugget the mole wants to find a gem big enough to fix his broken headlamp.
2. Pebbles rain with echoes; the wizard wears a miner's hat and builds tunnels.
3. Bats shove, minecarts roll, crystals duplicate, and the lights go out.
4. Nugget squints and walks into walls.
5. Finale: Geode, a sleeping rock golem, wakes up and wants to go back to sleep.
6. You out-stack him until he cracks open into a giant, very vain crystal bird. It lights the cave for Nugget, then flaps off, offended. Keepsake: a gem.

**10-beat arc**
1. **Torch Start**: Nugget lights the first torch; clear layers to bring the glow up.
2. **Gem Pockets**: gem-shaped holes in the rock; fill with the right piece.
3. **Minecart Rail**: a belt-lane minecart carries the stack along tracks; time drops.
4. **Bat Swarm**: cheeky bats shove pieces sideways when they flap by.
5. **Stalagmite Tower**: grow a stalagmite to the surface; Nugget peeks out at the top.
6. **Crystal Pattern**: fill a crystal cluster target; gems light up as they fill.
7. **Pitch Dark**: only a lantern circle shows the stack; Nugget carries the lantern.
8. **Cave-In**: falling rocks from the ceiling; survive 2:30.
9. **Echo Twin**: a ghost echo mirrors each piece; place both or clear them.
10. **Geode (transformation)**: the golem rocks the cave; each clear quiets him until he hatches into a crystal bird.
- **Bonus: Treasure Map**: fill an X-marked target with a fixed list; Nugget digs it up.

## 7. LAVA: Cinder's Snack

**Mini story**
1. Cinder the salamander wants to roast a marshmallow before the volcano erupts.
2. Ember hail falls; the wizard in a fire-proof cape builds an obsidian lookout.
3. Lava rises, geysers launch blocks, smoke fogs the view, and Cinder keeps getting offended.
4. Every snack Cinder gets near is roasted to charcoal by the volcano.
5. Finale: Smolder, a baby dragon, throws a tantrum because he secretly wants a bath.
6. You build a spa with water-blocks and dunk him in it. He sputters, refuses to admit he liked it and storms off. Cinder finally eats the marshmallow. Keepsake: an ember.

**10-beat arc**
1. **Warm Start**: Cinder lights the campfire; clear layers to stoke it.
2. **Ember Pockets**: glowing holes need the right pieces to cool them.
3. **Lava Lane**: a narrow island where the lava floor creeps up; keep ahead.
4. **Geyser Pop**: geysers launch spawned objects that land on the stack.
5. **Obsidian Tower**: build a heat-proof tower to the sign; Cinder fans himself on top.
6. **Marshmallow Roast**: fill the shape of a marshmallow on a stick; it browns as it fills.
7. **Smoke Screen**: smoke covers the stack for a few seconds after each eruption.
8. **Eruption**: the volcano rumbles and blocks trim; survive 2:30.
9. **Magma Flow**: the belt of magma slides the stack sideways; watch the gap.
10. **Smolder (boss duel)**: the baby dragon breathes fire and throws tantrums; build the spa and calm him.
- **Bonus: Forge Puzzle**: fill the anvil shape from a fixed list; Cinder hammers it.

## 8. CLOCKWORK: Tock's Midnight

**Mini story**
1. Tock the wind-up duck wants to deliver the last gear to the town clock before midnight.
2. Gears and springs rain; the wizard in brass goggles builds the clock tower.
3. Conveyors tick, springs boing, time stutters, and Tock runs down mid-step.
4. Tock stops mid-quack whenever he panics, and someone has to wind him back up.
5. Finale: Cuckoo Prime, the giant clock, wants midnight to stay forever and rings the hour to freeze you.
6. You build the last rung, the bell strikes twelve and the cuckoo is sprung out on its spring, sputtering. It retracts, still ticking smugly. Keepsake: a gear.

**10-beat arc**
1. **Cog Start**: Tock needs a base cog; clear layers to turn it.
2. **Gear Pockets**: gear-shaped holes need the right pieces.
3. **Conveyor Street**: the whole lane is a belt; plan for where the gap will be.
4. **Spring Surprise**: springs spawn and boing pieces upward.
5. **Clock Tower**: build the tower to the clock face; the hands tick as you climb.
6. **Pendulum Shape**: fill a swinging pendulum target.
7. **Stopwatch**: time freezes in bursts and pieces fall in jumps.
8. **Tick-Tock**: Tock runs down; survive 2:30 before the alarm.
9. **Rewind**: gravity reverses for a moment and blocks rewind upward.
10. **Midnight (escape)**: build your way up before the bell rings; Cuckoo Prime tries to freeze time.
- **Bonus: Music Box**: fill a music box with a fixed piece list; the tune plays as you fill.

## 9. NEON: Glitch's Gig

**Mini story**
1. Glitch the pixel cat wants to headline the city's neon gig.
2. Pixel rain falls; the wizard in a visor builds a stage and a skyscraper.
3. The beat drops pieces on rhythm, glitch cubes spawn, blackouts flicker, and the mirror world reverses the rules.
4. Glitch flickers and swaps places with itself.
5. Finale: DJ Mirrorball wants the crowd to dance forever and keeps spinning the song.
6. You clear on the beat and the last clear shatters his sparkle into confetti. Glitch's show begins while the ball, re-glued and smug, promises an encore. Keepsake: a neon star.

**10-beat arc**
1. **Boot Up**: Glitch boots the stage; clear layers to power it.
2. **Pixel Pockets**: pixel-art holes need the right pieces.
3. **Beat Lane**: pieces move on the beat of the music in a lane.
4. **Glitch Cubes**: glitch blocks spawn and swap places.
5. **Skyscraper**: build a skyscraper to the neon sign.
6. **Logo Shape**: fill a neon logo target; each cell lights in colour.
7. **Blackout**: the stack flickers in and out in time with the music.
8. **Lag**: everything slows and speeds randomly; survive 2:30.
9. **Mirror World**: left and right swap; the stage becomes a mirror.
10. **DJ Mirrorball (boss duel)**: clear on the beat to hit him; the final clear shatters the ball.
- **Bonus: Arcade High Score**: fill a high-score screen from a fixed list; Glitch plays it.

## 10. CELESTIAL: Comet's Bedtime (the Hat Reveal)

**Mini story**
1. Comet the fox wants to sleep on the tallest thing in the sky.
2. Meteor drizzle falls, and the wizard, whose hat now holds nine Keepsakes and is absurdly tall, builds a stair of stars.
3. Solar wind, eclipses and zero gravity throw the stack around; Comet trails sparkles and gets distracted.
4. The Sleepy Moon wants the same spot and yawns the stack sideways. The nine rival bosses heckle from the edge.
5. The tower starts to look oddly cone-shaped, with a familiar brim.
6. Finale: the last clear slots in the 10th Keepsake, and the camera pulls back: **the tower was the wizard's hat all along**. Every island of the campaign is a decoration on its brim. The wizard puts it on, Comet and every mascot pop out of the top, and the Moon is left in a sulk, vowing a rematch in the next season.

**10-beat arc**
1. **Starlight Start**: Comet lights the first star; clear layers to brighten it.
2. **Constellation Pockets**: star-shaped pockets need the right pieces.
3. **Solar Wind**: a long lane where solar wind pushes the pieces.
4. **Meteor Shower**: spawned meteors land on the stack as free blocks (or hazards).
5. **Orbit Tower**: build up to the orbit sign; mascots wave as you pass.
6. **Mascot Constellation**: fill the shape of each mascot in turn.
7. **Eclipse**: the moon blots out the stack for a moment.
8. **Zero-G**: pieces float; survive 2:30.
9. **Gravity Everywhere**: gravity points in every direction, one after another.
10. **The Sleepy Moon (big absurd twist)**: the Moon yawns the stack sideways and the rivals throw in their old tricks; the last clear seats the 10th Keepsake and reveals the tower as the wizard's hat.
- **Bonus: Hat Box**: pack the wizard's old, plain hat into its box with a fixed list; it no longer fits next to the new one.

**Seeding the reveal**: from Meadow on, the wizard's hat grows a little after each Keepsake, and finale towers lean slightly cone-shaped. Nobody points this out. Players should be able to rewatch any finale and spot the hat clues.
