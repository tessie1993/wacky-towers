## Merged blockers and fixes

1. **No layer can ever clear while the cage exists.** Rock cells count as active (`board_state.gd:199-201, 240-241`).
   - Fix: model the cage as masked-off columns (`static_geometry`).
   - Release a column with `request_mask(x, z, true)` (`rule_api.gd:294`, `board_sim.gd:1373`).
   - Owner: gameplay programmer with the systems designer.
2. **The first lock is an instant loss.** The 12-high walls count toward stack height against the limit of 8 (`board_state.gd:244, 525`; `rescue_top_out.gd:34-39`).
   - Masked columns fix this too.
3. **The fall time is wrong by about 2.5x.** `fall.g0` is in cells per second, so a piece takes about 18 s, not 7 s.
   - Redo the timer, the stars and the teaching beats.
   - Teach hard drop on the first piece, or raise `g0`.
4. **The pieces can't fill the pit.** By checkerboard parity, every stage needs an even number of T pieces, and the bag deals 1 T per 4 pieces. Spin-only rotation never gives a J.
   - Change the piece set: drop T, or add J.
   - Prove tiling with a solver: a 1-lookahead bot with no hold.
5. **The rescue text contradicts the code.** The spec says the top is scooped; the code wipes the bottom layers. The clock also freezes during the warning.
   - Align the spec and the GDD.
   - Delete the dead `grace_ms`.
6. **No camera or readability plan** for a pit sunk inside the walls, as the aspect changes per stage. Add a camera per stage, a wall fade, and a screenshot per stage.
7. **Compliance:**
   - Meadow loses its "funny" level.
   - The beat file is stale.
   - The meter key in `meadow_01.json:53` still says "Hearth Hum".
   - `story.objective` vs `objective_key` mismatch.
   - The stars are not derived from the plan formula.
   - No acceptance criterion for `verify_campaign_biomes`.
   - Three knots still read as a count of 3: consider a continuous grip ring.
8. **Smaller items:** kicks for an I piece in a 4-wide well, timer behaviour during the end beat, a placeholder left in the JSON diff, and reuse of `grip_release` in later levels.

**Status:** level 1's mechanics are on hold while the 12-level plan (`design/levels/levels.md`) is written. That plan will carry these fixes.
