## 4. Camera turn feel (a888910)

Read `src/view/camera_rig.gd` lines 37-50: the yaw snap now uses `TRANS_CUBIC` + `EASE_OUT` over `turn_anim_ms` = 150 (line 11),
instead of a linear tween; a running tween is killed first (line 42-43), and the logical yaw index changes at once.
- Good: ease-out gives a fast start and a soft landing, which reads much better than linear, and 150 ms is quick enough for a
  puzzle. Killing the old tween avoids fighting turns.
- Why 5.0 and not higher: there is no motion capture (a still cannot prove feel; capped at 6); a 30 degree snap in 150 ms with
  no overshoot, no parallax of the backdrop layers and no settle on the board is a competent default, not the polish of the
  reference boards. A strict panel would want the backdrop to turn at a different rate and a tiny settle. Also the TODO on
  line 39 (non-side-on snaps) is still open.
- Fix (camera owner / technical-artist): ease-out-back at about 180-200 ms with a 1-2% overshoot, backdrop layers rotating at 30-60%
  of the rate; done = a short retained frame strip of one turn in `production/qa/evidence/`.
