# World Foundation: Wacky Towers campaign lore

> **Status**: Draft v1 (world-builder, 2026-10-10), /team-narrative Phase 2, for narrative-director + user review.
> **Scope**: all 10 main biomes + 4 side islands: geography, island shapes per level band, critter communities, mascots, bosses (want, Mizzle's gift, how it arrived, the violet flag), materials, light per level, world rules, ambience, hidden clue props.
> **Canon sources, in precedence order**: user rulings 2026-10-10 (`production/session-state/decisions.md`, last two sections) > `design/gdd/narrative/meadow-story.md` + `characters.md` (Meadow is DONE, nothing here changes a beat) > `production/narrative/campaign-story/brief.md` (approved story bible) > `production/levels/campaign/biome-stories.md` > `production/levels/campaign/biome-bible.md` (old order and old casts, superseded per `contradictions-resolved.md` C2).
> **Canon Level key**: **E** = Established (stated in a source above). **P** = Proposed (this file, awaiting approval). Every table row carries one.
> **Visible To Player key**: **Y** = Yes (everyone sees it), **D** = Discoverable (backdrop, you have to look), **H** = Hidden (never shown on screen; it is design truth only).
> **Rules restated** (all E): wordless, no text anywhere; cute; nobody is hurt, only bonked, floured, dusted, rained on; bosses are rivals; Mizzle is shy, never scary; at most one thread clue per level, never two in one payoff, never over the grid; `tear` only for Mizzle, the Miller and Pip on a loss; mascots stay home, bosses roam; the nine later bosses appear in the Celestial finale skit as **guests only, never playable**; side islands + Scrapbook kept, built post-MVP; Cave low point = **softer** version.
> **No mechanics are decided here.** Anything with a gameplay implication is flagged to game-designer (section 9). No player-facing text here; flavour descriptions are design notes for the writer and art-director.
> **Every name, slot, colour and number in this file is a tunable default.**

Levels default to 5 + 1 bonus (tunable). Bands: **L1-2** introduce, **L3-4** complicate, **L5** finale, **B** bonus.

### 4.1 TUMBLE FAIR (carnival, after Candy)

- **A. Islands** (P): a striped-tent island with a ferris wheel. L1-2 a ring-toss stall and a helter-skelter base; L3-4 the funhouse mirror tent and the ferris wheel's cars; L5 **the big top ring** (landmark: Baron Balloon floating over the tents); B a prize-booth shelf. Settles into: sawdust with a scatter of confetti.
- **B. Community** (P): balloon animals (literal, they squeak), clown mice in tiny shoes, a ticket-taker goat, seals who juggle, toffee-apple bees. Stamp: a ticket.
- **C. Mascot** (E): **Bounce**, baby elephant, bounces on tents. Wants (P): to win the biggest prize (a giant plush wizard).
- **D. Boss** (E): **Baron Balloon**, a pompous hot-air balloon who wants to be the biggest thing at the fair. Bonk: deflates into a bouncy castle; everyone bounces on him (he secretly enjoys it). No gift.
- **Wink** (P): in L3's backdrop a violet cuff wins a tiny prize at the ring toss and slips away. **Charm** (E): a balloon.
- **E. Materials** (P): blocks the **Toy Box** set (painted wood, lacquer, stripes, dots; block-art-sets review decision 4); frames red-and-cream striped painted wood with painted (unlit) bulb studs and a tiny pennant.
- **F. Light** (P; brief "bright warm evening"): L1 golden evening, L2 golden evening, L3 sunset, L4 string-light twilight, L5 **fireworks night** (soft, slow bursts), B tent interior.
- **G. Ambience** (P): a far barrel organ (not the level's music), crowd laughter, balloon squeaks; bunting flutter and the slowly turning wheel in the backdrop.
- **H. Duck** (P): in a hook-a-duck pond under L1 and L2 (one duck is the real one).

### 4.2 DUNE BAZAAR (desert market, after Lava)

- **A. Islands** (P): sand islands in the volcano's dry lee, under striped awnings. L1-2 a spice stall and a sinking-sand yard; L3-4 a mirage plaza and a camel caravan dune; L5 **the sphinx steps** (landmark: Madame Sphinx lounging on a sand-carved sphinx of herself); B a merchant's tent. Settles into: smooth sand with a ripple line.
- **B. Community** (P): the **meerkat mob** (Tuft's family, pop up in turns), a sleepy camel, dung-free scarab beetles who roll sand marbles, lantern-selling tortoises, a jerboa rug merchant (no fennec fox: Comet is the fox). Stamp: a lantern.
- **C. Mascot** (E): **Tuft**, meerkat, pops up from holes. Wants (P): to trade one shiny pebble up into something wonderful.
- **D. Boss** (E): **Madame Sphinx**, a cat who poses picture riddles. Wants (P): someone clever enough to answer one. Bonk: solves her last riddle herself, is delighted, naps in the sun. No gift.
- **Wink** (P): in L2's backdrop a violet ribbon pennant hangs for sale on a stall (a spare flag offcut). **Charm** (E): a lantern.
- **E. Materials** (P): blocks the **Desert glazed terracotta** set (already in block-art-sets, homeless since the main chain has no desert); frames sun-bleached painted wood with a tile-inlay band and tassels.
- **F. Light** (P; brief "hot gold light, mirage"): L1 hot morning, L2 white noon, L3 white noon with heat shimmer (mirage), L4 amber afternoon, L5 **lantern sunset**, B tent shade.
- **G. Ambience** (P): market chatter as hums and calls (no words), wind over sand, camel snorts, lantern clinks; heat shimmer and drifting sand in the backdrop.
- **H. Duck** (P): for sale on a stall shelf under L1 and L2, with a price tag drawn as one coin.

### 4.3 BOO HOLLOW (spooky pumpkins, after Cave)

- **A. Islands** (P): pumpkin-patch islands in the forest's dusky shade, never scary. L1-2 a pumpkin patch and a lantern lane; L3-4 a cobweb attic and a haunted conveyor of rolling pumpkins; L5 **the cute haunted house's front lawn** (landmark: the house, its windows blinking like sleepy eyes); B the house parlour. Settles into: dark patch soil and a pumpkin sprout.
- **B. Community** (P): little ghosts in sheets of every size, pumpkin sprites (lantern-faced, giggle), friendly spiders who knit the cobwebs into doilies, a black cat who is afraid of everything, a bat or two (Glim's cousins; she waves if she is on the team). Stamp: a pumpkin.
- **C. Mascot** (E): **Wisp**, a little ghost in a sheet too big. Wants (P): to do one proper boo.
- **D. Boss** (E): **Sir Sheet-a-lot**, a ghost who wants an audience for his scares. Bonk: Wisp's boo "scares" him and he hides in a pumpkin. No gift.
- **Wink** (P): in L4's backdrop, one shape among the hanging sheets has a droopy pointed tip and a violet cuff, and is the only one actually startled. **Charm** (E): a bat.
- **E. Materials** (P): blocks the **plush_felt** party set (stitched seams, soft, like ghost sheets and pumpkin plush); frames slate-blue painted wood, cobweb doily corners, one tiny pumpkin. Keep purple **out** of frames and greyer than Mizzle's dusk violet in the sky (his wink must read).
- **F. Light** (P; brief "purple dusk with candle glow, never scary"): L1 pumpkin dusk, L2 candle twilight, L3 candle twilight, L4 **friendly full moon** (the Sleepy Moon dozes in the sky: a boss cameo), L5 lantern party, B parlour firelight.
- **G. Ambience** (P): soft giggles, a creaky door that creaks in tune, an owl-free hoot from a pumpkin sprite, spider-knitting clicks; drifting leaves and blinking windows in the backdrop.
- **H. Duck** (P): wearing a tiny ghost sheet under L1 and L2.

### 4.4 DRIZZLE ROCK (Mizzle's home, the epilogue, after Celestial)

> **Visible**: Y for the island; its history is D through the Scrapbook postcards. This is the only side island with story weight (E).

- **A. Islands** (P): a lone grey-violet rock at the far edge of the map, the speck on the horizon since the Meadow. A **leaning half-tower** (what is left after the Moon's yawn) and a **long set table for twelve** on a terrace. L1-2 the table end and the rain-puddle yard where he found his first block; L3-4 the leaning half-tower's stairs and the mail-cloud roost on its roof; L5 **the party terrace** (landmark: the half-tower, straightening a little each level); B the table itself, set again. Settles into: **a square of tablecloth** (the rock slowly becomes a party).
- **B. Community** (P): the **mail-cloud flock** (all the little violet clouds that came home, plus the grumpy cloud's cousins); **Crumb**; and by L5 the guests.
- **C. Mascot** (E): **the mail-cloud** (the grumpy cloud's cousin). Wants (P): to deliver one invite and see someone come.
- **D. Boss** (E): **Crumb**, the clanking butler, a party-loop machine that keeps setting the table. Origin (P): a wind-up from the Clockwork town that Mizzle wound up on the night of his first party; it has never stopped re-setting the table since. Wants (P): for the guests to arrive so it can finally sit. Bonk: sits down at last (E). No gift; a violet ribbon is tied round its winding key (wink).
- **Thread** (E): his stale party is re-set and this time the guests come. Guest list: see X9 (open).
- **Charm** (E): a violet cup.
- **E. Materials** (P): blocks the **candy_toy default set**, "unflavoured drizzle": the rock has no biome of its own, so the drizzle arrives plain, the way it was before it learned any place. Frames: grey-violet weathered wood made of mismatched patched planks (like his hat band) with one painted pale star.
- **F. Light** (E "soft, dusky violet then slowly warming"; P mapping): L1 grey drizzle, L2 violet dusk, L3 lamplight, L4 warming gold, L5 **warm sunrise party**, B candle table. The epilogue is the first time his rock sees warm light.
- **G. Ambience** (P): soft rain on stone at first, Crumb's clank and the clink of cutlery being set again and again; by L5 the rain stops, cutlery is joined by chatter-hums and cups clinking.
- **H. Duck** (P): sitting in one of the guest chairs under L1 and L2 (the first guest who ever came).

## 8. Notes for the writer and art-director

- **Speech and manner** (for the writer, wordless staging): Granny Mallow pats heads; Pebble bows before every slip; Puff inflates first, thinks later; Cinder huffs a spark before sulking; Chip gnaws when nervous; Nugget squints, then beams; Tock stops mid-step when he panics; Glitch flickers to show off; Comet drifts off mid-gesture. Bosses keep their surface emote (`smug`, `angry`, `gloom`) and never use Mizzle's secret ones, except at the finale's `question`, `exclaim`, `sweat`.
- **Every community stamps its own invites** (W3): good for backdrop dressing (doors, notice boards, parcels).
- **Palette guard** (art-director): no frame, prop or sky may borrow Mizzle's dusk violet except his own things (flags, cuffs, mail-clouds). Watch Cave crystals, Boo Hollow dusk and the Celestial nebula (X11).
