# Campaign story bible: "The Party Nobody Could Ask For"

> **Status**: Draft v1 (narrative-director, 2026-10-10), for user review
> **Scope**: the whole campaign: 10 main biomes (10 levels + 1 bonus each), 4 side islands, tournaments and minigames. Level lists are NOT here; level-designers work from this brief.
> **Binding canon**: wordless (skits, emote bubbles, props, map changes; no text anywhere); the player is the cloud wizard; the Meadow story is DONE (`design/gdd/narrative/meadow-story.md`) and its end state is our starting point; failure is a gag and nobody is hurt; bosses are rivals, not monsters.
> **Every name, order, beat, timing and number here is a tunable default.** Twists are pitched as directions for level-designers and game-designer, never as rules.
> **Anti-pillar respected**: story is skippable and never costs play time (section 5).

---

## 1. Premise and plot spine

### The idea in one paragraph

Every boss in the world has been pranking the wizard's towers because of **Mizzle**, a shy, lonely little rival wizard who wants only one thing: for the cloud wizard (and everyone) to come to his party. He cannot say it, cannot cast a proper block, and is too scared to knock on a door. So he mails **parcels**: each boss gets a party gift (icing bag, party horn, lantern chain, music record...) tied with a **violet ribbon flag** painted with one panel of a 10-panel picture invitation. Every boss misreads the gift as a weapon, a treasure or a toy, uses it for their own silly grievance, and ignores the flag. The pranks are the party games he meant to host. The wizard (and the player) think he is the villain. The Miller (Meadow) is the one who knows better: he has been lonely too.

### Why Mizzle is lonely (told only in optional postcards, section 2.4)

He once found a single block in the rain and built a tower for a party. He sent invitations by little mail-clouds; the clouds got lost, or were blown off course by the first pranks he never meant. Nobody came. The table stayed set for twelve. Since then he watches the cloud wizard's drizzle make friends everywhere and has been learning to cast, badly, in the background.

### The hidden thread players can follow wordlessly

| Motif | What it is | Where it shows |
|---|---|---|
| **Violet ribbon flag** | One pennant per biome, a painted pictogram panel (1 of 10) | On the boss's gadget in the payoff; then added to the map as bunting |
| **Star-stamped card** | An `invite` icon with a violet star stamp (Pip's invite is a leaf card with a basket stamp) | Held in the Miller's pocket next to Pip's leaf; in a few boss backdrops |
| **One more chair** | A table or snowman row set for guests, always one chair or cup too many | Ice (snowmen), Cave, Clockwork, finale |
| **Crooked tower** | Mizzle's own lopsided tower of mismatched blocks, growing on the map horizon | Island map after every finale |
| **Mail-cloud** | Tiny violet cloud with a satchel that delivers the parcels | Biome intros 8-9, boss payoffs, the grumpy-cloud twist (section 6) |

Players who ignore all of it get "a rival who pranks, then gets invited". Players who notice get an "I knew it" at the finale when the 10 panels line up.

### Plot spine: 11 beats

| # | Biome | Beat | What the attentive player sees |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Meadow | **Seed**: the Miller is redeemed. His lever has a violet bow; after the payoff a flag flutters on the mill roof beside the windmill | A second pennant-sized flag on the roof; a tiny crooked tower on the map's far edge |
| 2 | Candy | **First glimpse**: a tall, droopy hat in the bakery window, watching Mallow's party. A hand in a violet cuff takes one slice of cake | Silhouette only |
| 3 | Ice | **The snowman guests**: a ring of snowmen with one cup each, one empty chair. Lana finds them and knits scarves for them. First `tear` from an unseen source | The lonely tableau (the thread's emotional hook) |
| 4 | Underwater | **The misread**: the bottle map is really a picture invitation; Admiral Crab hoards it as treasure. Mizzle watches from a far cloud with a spyglass, ducks when noticed | First clear sight, from far away |
| 5 | Lava | **The Rosetta**: the Miller appears at the rim, points at the flag, then at the star card and leaf card in his pocket, then at the wizard. The player first understands "someone is sending these" | Mizzle seen walking away under a stack of parcels |
| 6 | Forest | **First contact**: the wizard and Mizzle reach for the same fallen acorn. He blushes, flees, and leaves a crooked tiny block as a gift: the first present that is not a prank | A gift; the wizard keeps it (map prop) |
| 7 | Cave | **Low point**: shadow puppets on the crystal wall show him playing with friends. He hears the wizard's group laugh (at a bat gag), thinks it is at his crooked block, tower collapses cutely, `tear` | His private world |
| 8 | Clockwork | **Midnight**: he waits at a table set for twelve until the clock strikes. Cuckoo Prime's "midnight forever" is a party that never ends because nobody came | The table |
| 9 | Neon | **The dance**: Mizzle in a glow-stick hat dances alone in the crowd; a circle forms; he flees, blushing. The wizard sends a wand-glint star after him: the first reply | First positive contact |
| 10 | Celestial | **The finale**: the Moon's yawn topples his crooked tower. The 10 flags are strung together by the bosses; the invitation reads in full. The Miller waves first. The wizard's tower is the party venue (and the hat). Mizzle is invited, sits | Everything lands |
| 11 | Epilogue | **Drizzle Rock** (his home island, post-game): his stale party is re-set; this time the guests come | Optional victory lap |

### How the finale redeems him

1. The Sleepy Moon yawns the stack; Mizzle's crooked tower topples into a harmless heap, with him on top and a drizzle cloud over him (`tear`).
2. The player's last clear seats the 10th Keepsake. The camera pulls back: the tower is the wizard's hat (existing reveal), and the mascots pop out of it.
3. The nine earlier bosses are on the brim. Each unrolls their flag (the Miller's is on top). The flags join into one bunting. The wizard reads the 10 panels: a small figure, a tower, a long table, everyone. The bosses look at their gadgets, then at Mizzle: `question`, `exclaim`, sheepish `sweat`.
4. The Miller does what he could not in Meadow: a full wave. Mizzle freezes (`dots` then `blush`).
5. The wizard (the player's wand glint) sends a block to the heap that becomes **a chair next to the wizard**. Mizzle sits. Pip, Mallow and the others pass him a cup. Everyone clinks.
6. The grumpy cloud (section 6) turns pastel and settles beside him. His droopy hat tip lights up its star. The star flies to the tip of the wizard's hat as the last map change.

Nobody is punished; the pranks were gifts nobody opened properly. The wizard's growth is that the player was the one who finally answered.

## 2. The rival wizard: Mizzle

### 2.1 Notes for the art-director (input, not decisions)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Silhouette** | Tall, thin and drooping, to contrast the wizard's small round upright shape. One glance: "the other wizard". Reads at a third of the screen as a bent-over question mark |
| **Hat** | Very long, floppy cone with the tip bent down and to the left. A single dim, unlit star sewn on the tip (it lights at the end). The band is patched with mismatched scraps |
| **Face** | No beard (opposite of the wizard). Huge round spectacles that reflect the scene; tiny nose; eyebrows and glasses carry the emotion |
| **Clothing** | Dusk-violet robe, sleeves too long, **violet cuffs** (the signature that identifies his hand in every glimpse), oversized scarf, small boots, satchel of parcels |
| **Cloud / vehicle** | Walks, or rides a small, thin, grey-violet cloud (a smaller, pointier cousin of the wizard's). The **mail-cloud** is separate: tiny, with a satchel |
| **Palette** | Dusk violet and pale gold. Keep 25 degrees or more from buff cyan, debuff and reward gold (art bible 4.4); the board colours are never violet-dominant |
| **Costume per biome** | None. His look stays constant so the thread is recognisable; only the glimpses change (a scarf in snow, a visor at the dance) |
| **Silhouette test** | Black-fill it next to the wizard, Pip and the Miller: three distinct shapes. His peak must stay readable behind a small prop (peeking) |

### 2.2 Tells, tics and emotes

- **Glasses push**: nervous. **Peek**: half behind a prop, one eye. **Counting on fingers**: counting guests. **Chair straighten**: sets a chair for a guest that is not there.
- **Wand doodling**: traces a block in the air that comes out lopsided, `sweat`. By biome 9 it comes out a clean cube.
- Never speaks. Never fights; he plays *near* the player, never at them.

| Mode | Emotes |
|---|---|
| Surface (when pranks land, for show) | `smug` (rare, a cover), `idea` |
| Real | `dots`, `tear`, `blush`, `sweat`, `heart` (finale only), `gift` (new, parcel with a ribbon), `invite` with the violet star stamp (variant of `invite`, no new id) |

### 2.3 Arc by biome (what he does, what he feels)

| Biome | Mizzle does | Feels | Clue strength (0-3) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Meadow | Nothing seen; flag on the roof | Hope | 1 |
| 2 Candy | Watches through a window | Wistful | 1 |
| 3 Ice | Sets up snowman guests | Lonely | 2 |
| 4 Underwater | Spyglass; caught; ducks | Anxious | 2 |
| 5 Lava | Walks away with parcels | Determined | 3 |
| 6 Forest | Gift exchange, flees | Brave then shy | 3 |
| 7 Cave | Shadow puppets, collapse | Crushed | 3 |
| 8 Clockwork | Waits at midnight | Patient | 2 |
| 9 Neon | Dances, gets a reply | Joy | 3 |
| 10 Celestial | Collapse, then invited | Seen | 3 |

### 2.4 Optional "memory postcards"

One wordless 3 s vignette per biome bonus level, shown in the island map's Scrapbook (not in-level, so no level skit is edited). Order is chronological: (1) finds a block in the rain; (2) builds a first small tower; (3) sets one table; (4) sends mail-clouds, which fly off; (5) waits; (6) the first prank blows his candles out; (7) he writes the 10-panel invitation; (8) cuts it into flags; (9) wraps ten parcels; (10) practises the cast in front of a mirror, then spots the cloud wizard far off. Missing every postcard still leaves the main story whole.

### 2.5 Finale payoff

See section 1. Afterwards he becomes a **friendly chaos host** in tournaments and hard-track remixes (the Miller's model: friendly rival, cheeky fist-shake, still a cameo prankster).

## 3. Biomes

Common to all: each biome has a boss who performs its twists as wind-ups (telegraphed, bonked and never hurt); the boss gets a parcel with a flag; mascots own their biome (the Celestial finale is the exception); the rubber duck hides in levels 1-2; all dials are defaults.

Format: Theme / Mood / Mascot / Boss / Thread / Twist directions / Minigame flavour / Gain / Finale payoff / Bonus / Map change.

### 1. MEADOW: Pip's Picnic (done)

- **Theme / mood**: turf over soil, daisies, a mill; golden daylight in three days (existing lighting).
- **Mascot / boss**: Pip the harvest mouse / the Miller (badger), redeemed.
- **Thread**: unchanged in-level. Additive props only: a violet-ribbon bow on his lever (visible only in close-ups: 05 spyglass, 10), and a flag on the roof in the post-10 map. Boss gift in lore: a pennant bow for the lever.
- **Twists** (existing): wind, belts, fog, flip.
- **Minigame flavour**: picnic raid, dandelion gusts, ant-line.
- **Gain**: keepsake tiny windmill (existing). Friend: none.
- **Finale payoff**: the redemption (existing). Bonus: Picnic Puzzle (existing).
- **Map change**: Miller at the picnic on the map; flag on the mill roof; Mizzle's crooked tower as a speck at the far map edge.

### 2. CANDY: Mallow's Birthday Cake

- **Theme / mood**: a floating bakery island: sugar cube, gumdrop, jelly, icing, a bakery shop with a lit window; warm morning-to-afternoon light, pastel, steamy.
- **Mascot**: Mallow the marshmallow bunny. Reacts with sticky bounces (sticks to the cake, peels off).
- **Boss**: Madame Meringue, a giant meringue who wants to be the cake topper.
- **Thread**: her gift is a piping bag meant for the party icing. She pipes herself a throne instead. A tall droopy hat peeks from the bakery window at Granny's party; a violet cuff takes one slice from the sill. Payoff: the flag is the ribbon on the bag.
- **Twist directions**: syrup lane (slow glide); jelly bounce; gumdrop hail that sticks; sugar rush speed; sideways chocolate fountain.
- **Minigame flavour**: cake decorating, gumdrop catch, syrup slide.
- **Gain**: keepsake cherry; no friend.
- **Finale payoff**: Meringue plops upside-down on top as topper, furious. Granny blows out the candles. A violet cuff leaves a thank-you pop (one tiny star).
- **Bonus**: Candy Box (existing). Mallow tries to eat one piece.
- **Map change**: cake with cherry on the island; flag-2 bunting from the bakery sign to the Meadow; the crooked tower gets a second floor.

### 3. ICE: Pebble's Egg Escape

- **Theme / mood**: snow, glacier ice, igloo bricks, pale blue, slow rolling clouds; cool morning that turns into a bright noon.
- **Mascot**: Pebble the penguin. Deadpan; bows to the player; slips with dignity.
- **Boss**: Big Sniffles the yeti with a cold.
- **Thread**: the gift is a party horn full of glitter. It makes him sneeze the avalanche. Footprints of a lone pair of small boots lead into the mountain. The player passes a ring of snowmen holding cups, one empty chair. First `tear`, from an off-screen source.
- **Twist directions**: slippery slide; rolling snowballs that grow; whiteout; thin ice that shrinks the board; cracks that flip the berg; finale as a sled escape.
- **Minigame flavour**: snowball roll, penguin slide, igloo race.
- **Gain**: keepsake snowflake. **Friend: Lana the alpaca joins** (section 4).
- **Finale payoff**: the sled lands home; Sniffles sneezes himself over the horizon, delighted. Lana, bundled in yarn, knits a scarf for each snowman in the ring.
- **Bonus**: Present Puzzle (existing). Pebble guards it.
- **Map change**: snowman ring on the glacier, all scarved; flag 3 at the summit; the tower grows a third floor.

### 4. UNDERWATER: Puff's Pearl

- **Theme / mood**: a coral reef: pastel coral, kelp, shell, a clam grotto; turquoise surface light that shifts to amber at sunset; bubbles.
- **Mascot**: Puff the pufferfish. Inflates at every surprise, then rises up and bumps the surface.
- **Boss**: Admiral Crab, a hoarder in a tiny hat ("for the fleet").
- **Thread**: the gift is a bottle containing a picture invitation. Admiral Crab reads it as a treasure map and hoards every shiny thing. The bottle goes into his pile; a violet-cuffed hand waves from a distant cloud above the surface, with a spyglass; ducks when the player's light finds him. Payoff: the flag is the tag on the bottle.
- **Twist directions**: slow sinking blocks; side currents; jellyfish springs; ink cloud; a whirlpool that spirals gravity; a claw that steals blocks.
- **Minigame flavour**: bubble pop, treasure dive, pearl relay.
- **Gain**: keepsake pearl; no friend.
- **Finale payoff**: the pearl pops into the clam; Crab shoots away in a bubble. The bottle floats up; Puff delivers it, inflated, to the surface cloud, where Mizzle hastily takes it and hides.
- **Bonus**: Treasure Chest (existing).
- **Map change**: reef lamp lit; flag 4 on a buoy; the tower now has a visible spyglass on a balcony.

### 5. LAVA: Cinder's Snack

- **Theme / mood**: obsidian, ash, magma veins, a volcano with a crater; deep red dusk into night; ember glow and smoke.
- **Mascot**: Cinder the fire salamander. Hot-headed; sulks when offended, huffs sparks.
- **Boss**: Smolder, a baby dragon who secretly wants a bath.
- **Thread**: the gift is a bath bomb set (meant as a party favour). He refuses it. The Miller appears on the rim, takes flag 5, waves it at the wizard, shows the leaf and star cards in his pocket, then points at the wizard: the **Rosetta** moment. Mizzle is seen walking away under a mountain of parcels.
- **Twist directions**: rising lava; geyser-launched objects; smoke screen; eruption trims; belt of magma; finale as a spa-building duel.
- **Minigame flavour**: marshmallow roast, lava hop, bath rush.
- **Gain**: keepsake ember. **Friend: Boulder the pygmy hippo** (section 4).
- **Finale payoff**: Smolder is dunked, sputters, refuses to admit he liked it. Cinder eats the marshmallow. The flag is hung up as a towel.
- **Bonus**: Forge Puzzle (existing).
- **Map change**: steam vents instead of fire; the Miller's mill gets a small pennant of its own; the tower gets a fourth floor and lantern.

### 6. FOREST: Chip's Bridge

- **Theme / mood**: mossy trunks, branch bridges, fireflies, a stream; green late-afternoon light that deepens into warm lantern dusk. The campaign's **breather** after the heat.
- **Mascot**: Chip the baby beaver. Eager, gnaws things that should not be gnawed.
- **Boss**: Grandpa Oak, a cranky tree who wants a nap.
- **Thread**: the gift is a set of wind chimes (party decorations). They wake him. **First contact**: when an acorn falls near the end, the wizard and Mizzle reach for it. He blushes, flees, and leaves a tiny crooked block as a gift.
- **Twist directions**: acorn showers; squirrel heist; firefly night; leaf wind; knocking woodpecker; sliding roots.
- **Minigame flavour**: acorn dash, bridge relay, firefly catch.
- **Gain**: keepsake acorn; no friend.
- **Finale payoff**: Oak blooms pink and becomes the bridge; the chimes ring in the blossoms. The tiny crooked block sits on the bridge post.
- **Bonus**: Berry Basket (existing).
- **Map change**: blossoms on the bridge; the crooked block keeps a corner of the hat's map icon; flag 6 as chimes; tower floor five, noticeably straighter.

### 7. CAVE: Nugget's Light

- **Theme / mood**: crystal, stalagmite, minecart rails, glow-moss; near darkness lit by lanterns and crystals. Lowest light of the campaign.
- **Mascot**: Nugget the mole in a headlamp. Squints, walks into walls, sneezes at dust.
- **Boss**: Geode, a sleeping rock golem who wants to stay asleep.
- **Thread**: the gift is a lantern chain (a disco for the party). It blinds Geode awake. Crystal walls cast shadow puppets of Mizzle playing with friends. The bats flap off and the wizard's group laughs; Mizzle's crooked tower collapses and he walks away in the dark, `tear`. The cuffs stay in view as a sad sleeve wipe.
- **Twist directions**: minecart belt; bats shoving; lantern-circle darkness; falling rocks; echo twins.
- **Minigame flavour**: lantern hunt, minecart relay, echo mimic.
- **Gain**: keepsake gem. **Friend: Glim the bat** (section 4).
- **Finale payoff**: Geode hatches into a vain crystal bird that lights the cave and flaps off offended. The light reveals Glim's blueprint on the wall; he joins.
- **Bonus**: Treasure Map (existing).
- **Map change**: crystals lit; flag 7 tied around a stalagmite; the tower is a leaning heap at the horizon (he has to start again), but with one new floor.

### 8. CLOCKWORK: Tock's Midnight

- **Theme / mood**: brass, gears, a clock tower, a wind-up town; lamp-lit sepia night; the clock face is the moon.
- **Mascot**: Tock the wind-up duck. Runs down when stressed; someone winds him up.
- **Boss**: Cuckoo Prime, a giant clock who wants midnight forever.
- **Thread**: the gift is a clock-stopper (so the party never ends). The cuckoo stops midnight. A table for twelve is set in a window, with one man waiting; each time the clock tries to strike, his chair is straightened. The mail-cloud delivers the parcel in the intro to level 8 or 9.
- **Twist directions**: conveyor streets; spring boings; stopwatch; gravity rewind; midnight escape.
- **Minigame flavour**: gear shuffle, tick-tock race, wind-up relay.
- **Gain**: keepsake gear; no friend.
- **Finale payoff**: the bell strikes twelve and Cuckoo is sprung, still ticking smugly. The clock face opens to show the table set for twelve, now with a new cup. The wizard places one cup.
- **Bonus**: Music Box (existing).
- **Map change**: clock chimes; flag 8 on a cuckoo door; the tower's table is set.

### 9. NEON: Glitch's Gig

- **Theme / mood**: a city of lit signs, stage, skyline; saturated night, pixel rain, beat-synced pulses. Highest energy of the campaign.
- **Mascot**: Glitch the pixel cat. Cool, flickers, shows off.
- **Boss**: DJ Mirrorball, a disco ball who wants the crowd to dance forever.
- **Thread**: the gift is a mixtape (a party playlist). He loops it. Mizzle is in the crowd in a glow-stick hat, dancing solo and nervously; the crowd forms a circle; he flees, blushing. The wizard's wand sends a star after him: the first reply.
- **Twist directions**: beat-lane timing; glitch cubes; flicker blackouts; lag; mirror world; rhythm duel.
- **Minigame flavour**: beat catch, dance-off, glitch swap.
- **Gain**: keepsake neon star; no friend.
- **Finale payoff**: the ball shatters into confetti; Glitch headlines. The mixtape plays properly, once. A glow-stick hat is left on a speaker.
- **Bonus**: Arcade High Score (existing).
- **Map change**: city lights up; flag 9 as a pennant on the stage; the tower is nearly straight.

### 10. CELESTIAL: Comet's Bedtime (the Hat Reveal)

- **Theme / mood**: indigo sky, nebula, marble, star stairs; the only biome that ends in **dawn**: the first sunrise of the campaign.
- **Mascot**: Comet the fox. Dreamy, trails sparkles; the finale gathers every mascot.
- **Boss**: The Sleepy Moon, who wants to sleep on the tallest thing (and is Mizzle's last parcel's target: a lullaby night-light).
- **Thread**: section 1. The Moon's yawn topples Mizzle's tower; the nine bosses hold their flags; the Miller waves first.
- **Twist directions**: solar wind; meteor drizzle; eclipse; zero-g; gravity from every direction; the nine rivals throw in old tricks as a boss-rush finale.
- **Minigame flavour**: constellation connect, comet dash, lullaby race.
- **Gain**: keepsake moon (existing plan); the star-tipped hat (last map change).
- **Finale payoff**: section 1, steps 1-6.
- **Bonus**: Hat Box (existing). Postcard 10.
- **Map change**: the whole map is the hat's brim; the full bunting runs through every island; Mizzle's chair sits by the wizard's cloud; the grumpy cloud is pastel.

## 4. Side islands (optional, off the main chain)

Four optional islands attach to the map. They are **self-contained gag islands**: no Mizzle plot beyond a single wink each (a violet cuff, a flag in the background), so they never gate or confuse the main thread. Default size: 5 levels + 1 bonus each (tunable; needs producer approval). Keepsake is a **cloud charm** (decorates the wizard's cloud, not the hat, so the hat count stays 10).

| Island | Unlock (default) | Theme | Mascot | Boss | Twist directions | Minigame flavour | Payoff / cloud charm |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Tumble Fair** (carnival) | after Candy (2) | Striped tents, pennants, a ferris wheel; bright warm evening | Bounce the baby elephant (bounces on tents) | Baron Balloon, a pompous hot-air balloon who wants to be the biggest thing at the fair | Wheel that rotates the board; ring toss that spawns pieces; funhouse mirror shape swaps | The tournament hub: ring toss, balloon pop, ticket race | Baron deflates into a bouncy castle; charm: a balloon |
| **Dune Bazaar** (desert market) | after Lava (5) | Sand, awnings, lanterns, a sphinx cat; hot gold light, mirage | Tuft the meerkat (pops up from holes) | Madame Sphinx, a cat who poses picture riddles | Sand that sinks pieces; mirage ghost blocks; riddle (fixed-list) puzzles | Treasure haggle, camel race, mirage hunt | She solves the last riddle herself and naps in the sun; charm: a lantern |
| **Boo Hollow** (spooky pumpkins) | after Cave (7) | Pumpkins, cobwebs, a cute haunted house; purple dusk with candle glow, never scary | Wisp the little ghost (a sheet too big) | Sir Sheet-a-lot, a ghost who wants an audience for his scares | Flickering visibility; phase blocks; haunted conveyor | Pumpkin toss, ghost tag, lantern hunt | He is "scared" by Wisp's boo and hides in a pumpkin; charm: a bat |

Drizzle Rock is the **epilogue island** (beat 11), the only side island with story weight. It repays the player who loved the thread but is not needed to finish the story.

## 5. The three new playable friends

Slots C2-C4; the cloud wizard is C1. `design/gdd/characters-perks.md` owns perks and the actual skills; each hook below is one line for the systems team to turn into a big, game-changing skill whose use rule (charge meter, once per round, consumable) depends on the mode. All are tunable defaults.

| | **Lana** (C2) | **Boulder** (C3) | **Glim** (C4) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Identity** | Alpaca knitter and scaffolder | Pygmy hippo demolition worker | Bat architect |
| **Joins** | Ice (3), finale payoff | Lava (5), finale payoff | Cave (7), finale payoff |
| **Silhouette hook** | Tall neck with a long yarn trail; needles in her hair. Differs from the round cloud wizard | Wide barrel body, oversized wooden mallet, hard hat with a lamp | Hangs upside-down, huge ears, rolled blueprint behind one ear |
| **Personality** | Calm, mothering, always busy; knits when worried | Cheerful, clumsy, enthusiastic; breaks things by accident, then fixes them | Fussy, perfectionist, excitable about angles; hates mess |
| **Emotes** | `heart`, `note`, `idea`, `blush`, `sweat` | `exclaim`, `sparkle`, `sweat`, `note`, `heart` | `idea`, `question`, `exclaim`, `angry`, `sparkle` |
| **Join beat** | Spots the lonely snowmen, knits them scarves, then offers a scarf to the wizard | Cracks the lava crust to free Cinder's snack; holds up the mallet to the wizard | Redraws the cave wall plan to show the way out; salutes the wizard |
| **Skill hook (one line)** | **Stitch**: pins the whole stack for a short time so twists cannot shift or trim it | **Smash**: removes a chosen chunk of the stack in one hit | **Redraw**: re-rolls the next few pieces and highlights perfect-fit spots |
| **Thread role** | She is the first to feel for the snowmen; her scarf is on the guest list | Her mallet is what smashes the bunting's last knot in the finale (comic relief) | Her blueprint of the tower is the plan for the Celestial hat |

Triad logic: **protect / remove / plan**, alongside the wizard's own casting. Each friend visits the biome mascots' world as a cameo only after joining (no mascot visits).

## 6. How story shows on screen

Extends the Meadow channels (`design/gdd/narrative/README.md`). Same rules: wordless; story at the rim and in the backdrop; never in the way.

| Channel | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Intro skit** | In the countdown, 3 s or less, ends on the hand-off: the mascot looks up, the wand glints, the first piece spawns |
| **Payoff skit** | 5 s or less, tap to skip. Biome finale 6 s on first play, the last 2 beats on replays |
| **Clue beat** | A glimpse of the thread (flag, card, cuff, chair) sits inside the payoff or the backdrop, at most once per level, at most 1.5 s, never over the grid, never during a warning |
| **Reactions** | As Meadow: one bubble at a time per character, at most 1 s |
| **Backdrop** | Ambient. Mizzle's props sit at the rim: the table, the snowman ring, the window |
| **Friend join** | At the biome finale payoff: a 4 s skit plus a wordless unlock card (silhouette to colour); tap to skip, the character is unlocked either way |
| **Island map** | Each won biome adds its bunting flag, its keepsake prop and its map change (per biome section); the crooked tower grows on the horizon |
| **Scrapbook** (new, menu) | A wordless gallery: the 10 flags (panels fill as won), the keepsakes, and the Mizzle postcards (earned in bonus levels). Ambient only; costs no play time |

### Rules every later writer must follow

1. **No words, ever.** No text in skits, cards, bubbles or the Scrapbook. If a beat needs a sentence, redesign it as a pose or a prop.
2. **Skippable and free.** Nothing in the story costs play time, gates progress, or demands attention. Missing every clue still gives a clean, happy story.
3. **Failure is a gag.** Nobody is hurt, ever; only bonked, floured, dusted, rained on.
4. **Mizzle is shy, not scary.** He never attacks, never looms, never gloats. Every glimpse is a small, sympathetic act. Nobody calls him a villain in the game; the wizard's friends only misjudge.
5. **The pranks are gifts.** A boss's prank is always a misuse of a party gift. Never stage a boss as truly cruel.
6. **`tear` is reserved**: only for Mizzle's and the Miller's lonely clues (and Pip on a lost level). No other character uses it.
7. **Clue density**: at most one thread clue per level, density 1 (biomes 1-2), 2 (3-4, 8), 3 (5-7, 9-10). Never two clues in one payoff.
8. **The wizard is you**: it reacts to the player's own play, never to Mizzle on its own; the friends react to the wizard.
9. **One disturbance near the board**: story motion never competes with the level's single disturbance.
10. **Mascots stay home**; only bosses cameo across biomes. Bosses never join the party, except the Miller (Meadow) and the single Celestial gathering (question 2).
11. **Same bubble for the same meaning** everywhere (`emote-bubbles.md`).
12. **Names, order and timings are defaults**; flag a change, do not silently apply it.
